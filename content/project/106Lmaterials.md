# Gradescope Submissions, Returns, and Resubmissions

All work for 106L will be submitted on [Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/courses/1199720). Here are some guidelines, links, and instructions for submission:

- Once you have completed your work, you need to scan it to a PDF. You should use a document scanner app on your phone: [Evernote Scannable on iOS (iPhone/iPad)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/evernote-scannable/id883338188), or [Genius scan on Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thegrizzlylabs.geniusscan.free&hl=en_US&gl=US). Adobe Scan works great too! Instructions for creating a PDF using these [can be found here](https://help.gradescope.com/article/0chl25eed3-student-scan-mobile-device).
- When logging into [Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/courses/1199720), be sure to use the 'School Credentials' button: ![login screen with school button highlighted](/gradescopelogin.png)
- (You can instead log into [Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/courses/1199720) via [Canvas](https://canvas.duke.edu/courses/68756) if you wish! That will log you in automatically.)
- After you've logged in, find the correct assignment in the Assignments area, and follow the [instructions here](https://help.gradescope.com/article/ccbpppziu9-student-submit-work#submitting_a_pdf). You must scan and submit all parts of every worksheet, as Gradescope will automatically assign questions to the right place. If you do not do this, your work may not be graded.
- Each question will be scored 1 (perfect), 0.9 (minor error), 0.5 (partly correct), or 0. Each question will get commented upon. You will then  use comments to correct your work and resubmit.
- Each assignment will have a separate resubmit [Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/courses/1199720) assignment: ![login screen with school button highlighted](/gradescopesubresub.png)
- After you resubmit, graders will regrade each question you ask them to  regrade by using the ‘Regrade’ checkbox below each question number. Once resubmissions are returned, you will get a ‘pass’ or ’no-pass’ on each  assignment. You will get an email from me if you did not pass an  assignment. Pass/no-passes will also be recorded in the Gradebook on [Canvas](https://canvas.duke.edu/courses/68756).

# Getting Help

You will need help with this class! Everyone will. Math at Duke is challenging, and this class will ask a lot of you, regardless of your math background. There are many resources available for help. Here's an outline with links:

- [Help Room](https://math.duke.edu/undergraduate/help-tutoring/help-rooms): Located in the Classroom building (Room 137), the help room is open around 25 hours a week: from 5-10pm Su-Th. It is staffed by TAs, graders, and others. You can go at any time to get help with your work. You can go do homework there and get help when you need it. It's a great space to have quiet work time, but have help readily available.
- **Office Hours**:  I will have two hours of in person office hours, and one hour of virtual office hours per week ([see hours above](#class-information)). Here's a [Zoom link](https://duke.zoom.us/j/96644572829) for the the virtual ones.
- **Individual meetings**: I'm available to meet one on one by Zoom if you feel that's needed. Drop me an [email](mailto:rann@math.duke.edu)! I'm also available for quicker questions before/after class.
- [The Academic Resource Center](https://arc.duke.edu/): Duke's ARC provides multiple layers of support for classes. From peer tutoring to study groups, this is one of the most useful resources Duke provides. Make an appointment with an advisor there, or sign up for a study group!
