Homework should be completed following the class on that day, before the next class. For example, Day 1-2 (01/09/19) homework should be completed before Day 1-3 (01/11/19). It is suggested that homework be done the day it is covered in class.

**Exception: Review day homeworks should be completed before that review day.**

Online worksheet and Lab PDFs are linked in the table below. Notation like '1.5/2,4,6' refers to chapter 1.5 of the textbook, questions 2, 4, and 6. 'Chapter 3 Review' refers to the review questions at the end of Chapter 3 of the textbook.

All homework from the textbook has solutions that can be found on [Sakai](https://sakai.duke.edu/portal/site/106lspring19) under 'Resources' -> 'Textbook Solutions'.
